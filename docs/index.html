Declared against derived

A plan declares its types; a consumer derives them again — or copies what the plan declared. When the two disagree, nothing in the format notices. Each cell below is one of 98 plans read by one of 9 implementations, compared against an expectation written from the spec rather than from any implementation.

An empty cell is silence, not a wrong answer. It means the implementation does not accept that plan at all — which is why silence is drawn as an outline and never as a colour.

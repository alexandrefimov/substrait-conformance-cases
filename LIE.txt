Who copies the declared type: probe/lie_matrix.sh, 2026-09-04.

Every declared output_type in the corpus is swapped for a false one (probe/make_lied_corpus.py:
decimal -> dec(20,2), i64 -> i32, i32 -> i64, bool -> i32, struct -> i64) and nothing else about the
plan changes. 23 of the 78 cases carry an output_type and are therefore touched.

  follows    - the answer matched the swap: the declaration was copied, nothing was derived
  changed    - the answer moved but not into the swapped type: it still depends on the declaration
  held       - the answer did not change: not copied from output_type
  NOTICED    - an error only on the swapped plan
  both fail  - the case is unsupported either way

The participants here are the ones whose probes run from one command. Spark and Gluten were in an
earlier hand-made version of this table and are not reproducible by the script, so they are gone.
